International standards for voice coil wire dimensions

Reference notes for basing wire_table.ods on established magnet-wire standards, matched against what the table already tracks (bare/overall diameter, resistance/m, mass/m, insulation build).

The core international standard: IEC 60317

IEC 60317 ("Specifications for particular types of winding wires") is the international reference for enamelled round copper wire. It's split into:

  • Part 0-1 / 0-2 / 0-3: general requirements — nominal bare-conductor diameter series (a Renard R20 preferred-number progression), diameter tolerances, and min/max overall (coated) diameter for Grade 1 (thin build) vs Grade 2 (thick build), roughly 0.02–1.6 mm depending on grade/part.
  • Numbered parts (e.g. -3, -8, -12, -13, -20...): specific enamel chemistries and thermal classes — polyurethane (class 130/155), polyester/polyesterimide overcoated with polyamide-imide (class 180/200), polyvinyl acetal (class 120), etc. Class 180/200 wires are the common choice for voice coils since they tolerate the heat a driver's motor generates.
  • Self-bonding variants (important for voice coils, since the winding is heat- or solvent-fused into a rigid self-supporting cylinder rather than glued): IEC 60317-35 (solderable polyurethane, class 155, w/ bonding layer), -36 (solderable polyesterimide, class 180, w/ bonding layer), -37 (polyesterimide, class 200, w/ bonding layer), -38 (dual-coated, class 200, w/ bonding layer).

Regional adoptions of this same standard: EN 60317 (Europe, identical text), GB/T 6109 (China), and it's closely harmonized with JIS C3202 (Japan) — Elektrisola's published datasheet is explicitly framed as "based on IEC 60317, based on JIS 3202" and gives real numeric bare/overall-diameter, resistance/m and mass/m tables per size, which is a good ready-made cross-reference if the IEC document itself (paywalled, numeric tables not public) is hard to get hold of.

North American alternative: NEMA MW 1000 (ANSI/NEMA MW 1000)

Sizes wire by AWG rather than a metric diameter series, with Single / Heavy / Triple / Quad film-build classes (Heavy ≈ 2× the insulation increase of Single). This is what most US magnet-wire suppliers (MWS Wire, Superior Essex) quote against. If you want your table indexed by AWG instead of µm, this is the standard to follow instead of IEC 60317.

For CCAW entries specifically

The CCAW rows (copper-clad aluminium) aren't plain copper, so the base-metal geometry is covered separately: GB/T 29197-2012 (China) and ASTM B566 are the specs usually cited for CCA wire construction, while the enamel/insulation build on top of it still follows the same IEC 60317 (or NEMA MW 1000) grade system as copper wire.

Practical recommendation

Since the existing table is already vendor-catalog-shaped (named sizes in µm, w_avg/ w_max/resistance/mass_density), the cleanest path is: use IEC 60317-0-1's Grade 1/Grade 2 diameter series as the backbone (so nominal_size steps match the actual preferred sizes wire is manufactured in), and cross-check bare/overall diameter, resistance and mass-per-length against a real supplier datasheet built to that standard — the Elektrisola PDF below is a solid one to compare against — rather than trying to buy/transcribe the IEC document itself.
